## 4. Full App Store & Google Play Description (PAS Framework)

### PROBLEM: Independent Clinics Leak High-Margin Patients Every Day—And Agencies Pitch Blind
Independent dental and healthcare clinics across Greater Noida and Delhi NCR lose 30–40% of high-intent Google Maps patients before the phone ever rings. Broken SSL links, abandoned subdomains, virtual PBX call drops, and unconfirmed evening OPD slots push ₹25,000+ Dental Implant and ₹65,000+ Aligner patients straight into corporate chains. Meanwhile, agency founders waste hours sending generic cold messages without knowing what each clinic can afford.

### AGITATION: Generic Outreach Gets Ignored, and Slow Proposals Kill Deals
When a doctor finally replies "Send me the price," fumbling with spreadsheets or generic PDFs kills momentum. Without local market-rate benchmarks in ₹ INR and procedure-level ROI anchors, your outreach looks like spam instead of a clinical growth audit.

### SOLUTION: Indian Bison — The Local Lead Engine, Quotation Studio & WhatsApp CRM
Indian Bison transforms raw Google Business Profile audits into closed retainers with real-time Cloud Firestore sync (`asia-south2` Delhi):

- **20 Pre-Audited NCR Dental Prospects (`₹14.41L` 12-Month LTV):** Ranked by urgency score (65–98/100) with exact operational friction points verified.
- **4 Psychological WhatsApp Hooks (`<60 Words`):** Switch between Value-First, Pain-First, Peer Social Proof, and Low-Friction Micro-Offer hooks with built-in `(Reply STOP to opt out)` compliance.
- **1-Click ₹ INR Quotation Studio (`Alt+Q`):** Pre-loaded with Greater Noida / Delhi NCR market rates (`₹9,500` to `₹38,000` setup + `₹2,500` to `₹7,500/mo` retainers) and procedure-based ROI anchors (e.g., *"1 Single Dental Implant recovers 100% of setup cost"*).
- **Desktop & PWA Speed Hotkeys:** Navigate prospects (`Alt+↑/↓`), switch hooks (`Alt+1..4`), load itemized ₹ quotations (`Alt+Q`), and launch WhatsApp Web/App (`Alt+W`) in seconds.
- **PostHog + GA4 Conversion Telemetry:** Every outreach click, quotation load, and pipeline stage transition is tracked in real time.
